How EWA works with payroll

The role of payroll data, employer rules, cycles and deduction schedules.

Payroll provides the data foundation for responsible EWA: employee identity, eligibility, net pay, payroll cycle and deduction capacity.

WDAS can support secure file exchange or API integration depending on the payroll environment and implementation scope.

After access, the operating platform generates payroll deduction schedules and manages reconciliation and exceptions.

WDAS perspective: EWA works best as a controlled, payroll-native operating model rather than an isolated app or generic cash-access product.

Key operating components

  • Employer-approved rules and eligibility
  • Secure payroll data handling
  • Consent, status and audit trail
  • Payment and settlement workflows
  • Deduction schedules and reconciliation
  • MIS and responsible-use insight
