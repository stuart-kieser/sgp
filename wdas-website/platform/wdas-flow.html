WDAS Flow™

The EWA operating engine that manages employer rules, employee eligibility, accessible value calculations, transaction workflow, consent, payout routing and payroll deduction outputs.

The problem

What breaks without WDAS Flow

Without a controlled rules and workflow engine, EWA can become inconsistent, difficult to reconcile and operationally risky.

Where it fits

WDAS Flow™ forms part of the WDAS operating stack, connecting employer rules, payroll data, transactions, payments, recovery and management information.
